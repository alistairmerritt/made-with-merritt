Getting started

Get Second Chair working.

See and control the music playing on your Mac, right from your iPhone’s Lock Screen, Control Centre or StandBy.

What you’ll need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon, running macOS 27, with Apple’s Music app
  • An iPhone, running iOS 27
  • Second Chair for Mac, and a TestFlight invitation for the iPhone app. Ask for one
  • An internet connection on both. They don’t need to share a network.

You don’t need Apple Music to see and control your Mac’s music. You do need it on your iPhone for Continue Listening.

Part 1

Get connected

Four simple steps to connect your Mac and iPhone.

  1. MacInstall on your Mac
  2. iPhoneInstall on your iPhone
  3. Mac and iPhonePair your devices
  4. Mac and iPhoneShow what’s playing

01

On your Mac

Install on your Mac

Download Second Chair, move it to Applications and open it.

Download for Mac Version 0.3.8

When your Mac asks whether Second Chair can control Music, click OK. That’s how it sees what’s playing.

Second Chair lives in your menu bar, as a music note . Click it to see everything it does.

More detail
  • Can’t see the music note? Look at the top right of your screen, next to the clock. If your menu bar is full, it may be hidden behind other icons.
  • Start it with your Mac. In the music note’s menu, turn on Launch at Login. Second Chair needs to be running to update your iPhone.
  • Clicked Don’t Allow? The menu will say Second Chair can’t read Music. Click Open Privacy Settings, then turn on Music under Second Chair.

02

On your iPhone

Install on your iPhone

Open your TestFlight invitation, install Second Chair, then open it and tap Continue.

You’ll see Pair with Mac. Leave it open for the next step.

No invitation yet? Ask for one.

More detail
  • New to TestFlight? It’s Apple’s free app for trying apps before they’re released. Install it from the App Store if you don’t have it, then open your invitation again.
  • Which version? You’ll need build 202611191034 or later.

03

On your Mac and iPhone

Pair your devices

Your Mac gives you a code. Type it on your iPhone, then allow the connection on your Mac.

  1. MacClick the music note and choose Pair iPhone…
  2. iPhoneEnter the six-digit code and tap Pair.
  3. MacWhen the menu says your iPhone wants to show this Mac’s music., click Allow.
More detail
  • Code expired? A code lasts five minutes and works once. Choose Pair iPhone… again for a new one.
  • One iPhone per Mac. You can pair one iPhone with each Mac.

04

On your Mac and iPhone

Show what’s playing

Play something in Music on your Mac, then tap Show in Now Playing in Second Chair on your iPhone.

This final step brings Second Chair to your Lock Screen.

More detail
  • Button greyed out? Show in Now Playing waits until music is playing on your Mac. Start a song in Music, and it lights up.
  • Second Chair doesn’t open Music for you. Open it yourself on your Mac.

You’re ready.

Lock your iPhone. The song playing on your Mac is right there, with its artwork and controls.

  • Lock Screen
  • Control Centre
  • StandBy
  • Apple Watch

Play, pause, skip and scrub from any of them, and change Music’s volume on your Mac. The sound stays on your Mac.

It works away from home too, as long as both devices are online and your Mac is awake with Second Chair running.

Part 2 · Optional

Take your music with you.

Heading out? Pick up where you left off on your iPhone. When you’re back at your Mac, bring your music back.

Continue Listening needs an Apple Music subscription on your iPhone. Everything else works without it.

  1. Mac · onceConnect to Music
  2. iPhoneContinue on iPhone
  3. iPhone or MacContinue on Mac

01

On your Mac · once

Connect to Music

Click the music note and choose Connect to Music… In Music, select Second Chair under Devices and enter the four-digit code.

If your Mac asks about the local network, allow it. This is a separate code from pairing your iPhone. When it’s done, the iPhone app shows Ready under Continue Listening.

More detail
  • Why the local network? Second Chair uses it to talk to Music on your Mac. Nothing leaves your Mac.
  • Home Sharing isn’t needed.
  • Music says the code is wrong? Click Cancel, choose Connect to Music… again, and pick the Second Chair entry that has just appeared under Devices.

02

On your iPhone

Continue on iPhone

Open Second Chair and tap Continue on iPhone.

Your Mac pauses, and your music picks up on your iPhone where you left off. The first time, allow Second Chair access to Apple Music.

03

On your iPhone or Mac

Continue on Mac

Back at your desk? Tap Continue on Mac on your iPhone, or choose Continue from iPhone in the music note’s menu.

Your Mac picks up where you left off, and your iPhone pauses.

More detail
  • Don’t see Continue from iPhone? It appears when Music on your Mac is paused or stopped.
  • Your Mac asks you to confirm? Tap Continue on Mac in the notification on your iPhone or Apple Watch. Notifications need to be on for Second Chair.

Apple Music works a little differently on Mac and iPhone, so some things, like shuffle order and stations, can change when you switch. See what comes with you
