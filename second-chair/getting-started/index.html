Getting started

Get Second Chair working.

See and control your Mac’s music from your iPhone’s Lock Screen, Control Centre and StandBy.

What you’ll need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon, running macOS 27, with Apple’s Music app
  • An iPhone, running iOS 27
  • Second Chair for Mac, and a TestFlight invitation for the iPhone app. Ask for one
  • An internet connection on both. They don’t need to share a network.

You don’t need an Apple Music subscription to see and control playback on your Mac, but you’ll need one to use Continue Listening on your iPhone.

Part 1

Get connected

Four steps to get your Mac’s music onto your iPhone’s Lock Screen.

  1. MacInstall on your Mac
  2. iPhoneInstall on your iPhone
  3. Mac and iPhonePair your devices
  4. MacShow what’s playing

01

On your Mac

Install on your Mac

Download Second Chair, move it to Applications and open it.

Download for Mac Version 0.3.9

When your Mac asks whether Second Chair can control Music, click OK. That’s how it sees what’s playing.

Second Chair lives in your menu bar, as a music note . Click it to see everything it does.

More detail
  • Can’t see the music note? Look at the top right of your screen, next to the clock. If your menu bar is full, it may be hidden behind other icons.
  • Start it with your Mac. Second Chair offers Launch at Login once your iPhone is paired. You can also turn it on in the music note’s menu: click the gear (⚙︎), then Launch at Login. Second Chair needs to be running to update your iPhone.
  • Clicked Don’t Allow? The menu will say Second Chair can’t read Music. Click Open Privacy Settings, then turn on Music under Second Chair.

02

On your iPhone

Install on your iPhone

Open your TestFlight invitation, install Second Chair, then open it and tap Get Started.

The iPhone app walks you through each step, with help on every screen. If Second Chair is already on your Mac, tap It’s Already on My Mac.

No invitation yet? Ask for one.

More detail
  • New to TestFlight? It’s Apple’s free app for trying apps before they’re released. Install it from the App Store if you don’t have it, then open your invitation again.
  • Which version? You’ll need build 202611241054 or later.

03

On your Mac and iPhone

Pair your devices

Second Chair shows a pairing code on your Mac. Type it in Second Chair on your iPhone, then allow the connection on your Mac.

  1. MacClick the music note and choose Pair iPhone…
  2. iPhoneType the six-digit code.
  3. MacWhen the menu says your iPhone wants to show this Mac’s music., click Allow.
More detail
  • Code expired? A code lasts five minutes and works once. Choose Pair iPhone… again for a new one.
  • One iPhone per Mac. You can pair one iPhone with each Mac.

04

On your Mac

Show what’s playing

Open Music on your Mac and play a song. Second Chair brings it to your iPhone’s Lock Screen.

More detail
  • Second Chair doesn’t open Music for you. Open it yourself on your Mac.

You’re ready.

Lock your iPhone and you’ll see what’s playing on your Mac, complete with artwork and playback controls.

  • Lock Screen
  • Control Centre
  • StandBy
  • Live Activities
  • Apple Watch

Use these controls to play, pause, skip, scrub or change Music’s volume on your Mac. The sound stays on your Mac.

It even works when you’re away from home, provided both devices are online and your Mac is awake with Second Chair running.

If your Mac starts playing after a break, a Live Activity with its controls may appear first. Tap it to bring back the full player.

Part 2 · Optional

Take your music with you.

Heading out? Pick up where you left off on your iPhone. When you’re back at your Mac, bring your music back.

Continue Listening needs an Apple Music subscription on your iPhone.

  1. Mac · one-time setupConnect to Music
  2. iPhoneContinue on iPhone
  3. iPhone or MacContinue on Mac

01

On your Mac · one-time setup

Connect to Music

This is a separate connection from pairing your iPhone. You’ll enter a four-digit code in Music on your Mac, rather than the six-digit pairing code in Second Chair on your iPhone.

  1. MacIn Second Chair, click the music note and choose Connect to Music…. If you chose Not Now earlier, it’s in the gear (⚙︎) menu.
  2. MacIn Music, select Second Chair under Devices and enter the four-digit code shown in Second Chair.

If prompted, allow local network access. Once connected, Continue on iPhone appears in the iPhone app.

More detail
  • Why the local network? Second Chair uses the local network permission to communicate with Music on your Mac. This connection stays on your Mac.
  • Home Sharing isn’t needed.
  • Music says the code is wrong? Click Cancel, choose Connect to Music… again, and pick the Second Chair entry that has just appeared under Devices.

02

On your iPhone

Continue on iPhone

Open Second Chair and tap Continue on iPhone.

Your Mac pauses, and your music picks up on your iPhone where you left off. The first time, allow Second Chair access to Apple Music.

03

On your iPhone or Mac

Continue on Mac

Back at your desk? Tap Continue on Mac on your iPhone, or choose Continue from iPhone in the music note’s menu.

Your Mac picks up where you left off, and your iPhone pauses.

More detail
  • Don’t see Continue from iPhone? It appears when Music on your Mac is paused or stopped.
  • Your Mac asks you to confirm? Tap Continue on Mac in the notification on your iPhone or Apple Watch. Notifications need to be on for Second Chair.

Apple Music works a little differently on Mac and iPhone, so some things, like shuffle order and stations, can change when you switch. See what comes with you

Want it to happen by itself? Use Shortcuts to take your music with you when you connect to CarPlay, or bring it back when your headphones connect.
